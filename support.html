Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Premium subscription?

Go to Settings → Premium → Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

The widget isn't showing my pinned quote.

Premium is required to use the widget. Ensure you have an active subscription, and that your pinned quote is from Favorites, Bookmarks, or My Quotes.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Go to Settings → Premium → Manage Subscription, or open iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions and select Daily Wisdom.

Can I use the app without Premium?

Yes! All core features (browsing quotes, favorites, bookmarks, reflections, and daily notifications) are free. Premium unlocks My Quotes, collections, OCR scanning, and the widget.

How do I add my own quotes?

With Premium, go to My Quotes in the sidebar, tap the + button, and choose to type or scan a quote from a book.

Is my data backed up?

Currently, data is stored locally on your device. iCloud sync is on our roadmap for a future update.
